# API Map

## Payroll adjustments

Apply `node --env-file=.env scripts/apply-payroll-adjustments.mjs` before using these routes; it installs `database/payroll-adjustments.sql` and `database/payroll-adjustment-manual-days.sql`. `payroll_adjustment` stores the employee, original DTR, later target DTR, workflow status, audit users/timestamps, and calculated net adjustment. `payroll_adjustment_line` snapshots each saved original attendance component with its date, quantity, payroll rate, direction, and amount. Attendance remains attached to its original DTR and is never rewritten by this workflow. The unique `(SourceAttendanceID, ComponentCode)` key prevents the same saved attendance component from being carried into payroll twice. A rejected or cancelled adjustment can be reopened instead of recreated. `payroll_adjustment_manual_day` records each verified missing date, chosen shift, and entered hours with a unique active employee/source-cutoff/date claim. Rejected/cancelled claims are released while their historical rows and amounts remain available; reopening conflicts with a newer claim. Manual days are limited to ordinary non-Sunday dates; holiday/rest-day premiums need a separate reviewed calculation. `Applied` and `TargetPayrollID` are reserved for the payroll-generation stage; approval alone does not post the amount to payroll. Once applied, the record is immutable through these endpoints and a later correction must use a reversal adjustment.

The Daily Time Records list exposes **Payroll adjustments** under each row's **More** menu. The selected DTR is the target cutoff where the correction will be paid. After selecting the employee, the **Original cutoff** dropdown lists only earlier same-agency DTRs where that employee has saved payable attendance. Selecting a cutoff retrieves only its payable saved attendance dates; the user chooses the missed dates to carry forward. `app/components/PayrollAdjustmentsPanel.vue` is shared by this DTR modal and the Payroll → Adjustments review page. Desktop uses a table with expandable component lines; mobile uses cards. Viewer accounts are read-only. Encoder accounts can create, submit, cancel, and reopen records. Admin and Supervisor accounts can additionally approve or reject submitted records.

## Rates

**DTR Excel blank biometric rule.** A row with an employee and date but without both `Time In` and `Time Out` is treated as no attendance, even if it has a shift code. It leaves that DTR day blank and, during re-import, clears only a prior attendance row for that same DTR employee/date.

All rate routes are session-protected. `:resource` is whitelisted to `payroll-rate`, `billing-rate`, or `site-rate`.

**Rate amounts.** Apply `database/rate-ot-extension-deductions-wdo.sql` once before deploying the updated rates forms/API. It adds `OTExtRate`, `RestDayOTRate`, `LateDeduction`, and `UndertimeDeduction` as `DECIMAL(10,2) NOT NULL DEFAULT 0.00` to both `payroll_rate` and `billing_rate`. Existing rate amounts are preserved; zero is the initial unconfigured amount, with no inferred rate or multiplier. `RestDayRate` is displayed as WDO / Rest day; `RestDayOTRate` is its separate WDO OT amount. Late and undertime deduction fields are editable and visible in rate forms/previews, in PHP per hour. Their quantities use minutes / 60 when payroll deduction computation is implemented; deductions must be applied only once. This change stores configuration only; it does not deduct DTR hours, create payroll posting logic, or rewrite historical RP rate snapshots.

# Database recovery

**DTR deduction stage.** Scheduled DTR hours are gross before late/undertime deductions. For example, DJA `07:23–18:02` on `07:00–19:00` keeps 8 regular + 4 OT, 23 late minutes and 58 undertime minutes. This contract applies to explicit form recalculation and Excel import; existing saved allocations are not silently rewritten on read. Re-enter an actual timestamp and save, or re-import the source, to recalculate an old Draft row. Payroll must apply these deductions once downstream, with the appropriate regular/OT rate allocation. Currently `POST /api/attendance/dtr/:id/compute` only changes DTR status; it does not create payroll records or calculate monetary deductions. Request/response shapes and tables are unchanged.

**DTR late/undertime timestamps and Excel round-trip.** In `app/components/DtrAttendanceWorkspace.vue`, explicit Late-minute input on a scheduled shift sets Time In to the scheduled start plus those minutes; Undertime-minute input sets Time Out to the scheduled end minus those minutes, with overnight date handling. Hours, ND, and status then recalculate. Editing duration hours also refreshes late/undertime from the resulting timestamps. Loading a saved row never adjusts timestamps. Scheduled undertime is measured against the **whole shift end**, including its scheduled OT block, in both the form and Excel importer. Example: `07:00–19:00` with 24 minutes undertime becomes `07:00–18:36`, 8 regular hours, 4.00 OT, and 0.40 UndertimeHours; 56 minutes late sets Time In to `07:56`. Late and undertime remain separately tracked deductions; neither reduces scheduled Regular/Regular OT in the DTR. A worked scheduled duty (including referenced augmentation) keeps its configured allocations, with the site break still applied once. Actual extension and ND remain based on actual timestamps. Re-entering the gross scheduled duration preserves recorded undertime; an explicit different duration still previews its resulting timestamps and metrics. Standalone Flexible or consolidated multi-duty rows have no single scheduled start/end for minute-to-time conversion.

`GET /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records` returns corrected `dutyRows` for Excel export: zero/one-duty dates use the saved `attendance.TimeIn`, `attendance.TimeOut`, and saved shift; dates with multiple biometric duties retain each `attendance_duty` line and its shift. Tables read: `attendance`, `attendance_duty`, `shift_code`, `employee`. The `POST .../records` manual-save and import request/response shapes are unchanged. The export retains its eight existing columns; late/undertime are represented by Time In/Time Out rather than separate minute columns. Single-duty manual corrections therefore survive export and re-import without rewriting the original imported duty. Export reads saved records; unsaved form edits require Save attendance first.

**Automatic DTR Late / Half-Day status.** `GET /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records` and attendance write actions on `POST /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records` (manual save, import, batch shift, clear/reset) synchronize `attendance.AttendanceStatus` for Draft batches using `attendance`, `shift_code`, and `attendance_duty`, within the existing transaction. Existing computed/approved/locked history is unchanged. `attendanceRows` also includes `ShiftRegularHours` (the selected shift's regular-hours baseline; maximum source-duty baseline for consolidated rows). Request shapes are unchanged. The caller is `app/components/DtrAttendanceWorkspace.vue`; its live form and backend share `shared/utils/dtrAttendanceStatus.ts`.

Half-Day applies when positive worked time is at most half of the configured **regular hours**, excluding the OT cap: 4 hours or less for an 8-hour regular block. Classification uses actual timestamp duration minus the saved break, capped by credited hours minus late; without timestamps it uses credited hours minus separately tracked late and undertime. These subtractions classify status only and do not reduce stored DTR hour allocations. ND and mirrored holiday allocations do not count twice. Half-Day takes precedence over Late; otherwise positive late or undertime hours produce the shared Late status, and clearing both returns attendance to Present (or retains Holiday). The form monitors both Late and Undertime minute inputs immediately, and the same rule applies to imported/saved rows. Explicit Absent, Rest Day, On-Leave, and Reliever statuses remain unchanged. This classification does not rewrite timestamps, hour amounts, rate snapshots, WDO rules, or Total Days. The grid adds a 3px orange bottom line for Late or positive Undertime hours and a black bottom line for Half-Day (which takes color precedence), with a Late / Undertime legend and a tooltip showing undertime minutes, while retaining the existing shift backgrounds and holiday headers.

**DTR straight-duty display rule.** `GET /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records` returns `IsStraightDuty: 1` when one employee/date contains both a scheduled duty and a Flexible augmentation duty in `attendance_duty`. `DtrAttendanceWorkspace.vue` renders that date as green `SS`; the raw duties and calculated payroll hours are unchanged.

**Site-controlled WDO and Special Holidays.** Apply `database/dtr-site-wdo-special-holiday-policy.sql`, or run `node --env-file=.env scripts/apply-dtr-site-wdo-special-holiday-policy.mjs`, before deploying these controls. The migration adds `site_policy.AutoWDOEnabled` (default on), `site_policy.SundayWDOOTEnabled` (default off), and the `site_special_holiday` site-to-holiday mapping table. Existing active Special Holidays are initially mapped to active sites to preserve prior DTR behavior; later user changes are retained. The DTR and compact print summaries expose only **WDO** and **WDO OT**. The existing attendance form displays the persisted payroll allocation in read-only `RestDayHours` and `RestDayOTHours`: automatic WDO uses fixed 8/0 hours, while Sunday WDO uses actual regular hours and OT plus OT extension.

**DTR holiday credit rule.** `GET /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records`, attendance saves/imports/batch apply, and Draft compute synchronize holiday allocations inside their existing transaction (`attendance`, `attendance_dtr`, `attendance_dtr_employee`, `holiday`, `site_special_holiday`; caller `app/components/DtrAttendanceWorkspace.vue`). A worked Legal Holiday or site-enabled Special Holiday retains a one-per-calendar-day allocation: its holiday hours mirror `RegularHours` up to 8, and its holiday OT mirrors `OTHours` up to 4. `OTExtHours` is excluded from holiday OT. When the site's Sunday WDO OT setting is on, a worked Sunday that is an enabled Special Holiday posts those 8/4-capped values to `RestDaySpecialHolidayHours` and `RestDaySpecialHolidayOTHours` instead of the ordinary Special Holiday fields; a worked Sunday Legal Holiday still posts to `LegalHolidayHours` and `LegalHolidayOTHours`. With the setting off, ordinary holiday fields apply. Sunday WDO OT itself may include OT extension separately. The day editor previews these read-only holiday fields immediately, and its row/totals preview uses the same values before Save. No holiday OT is generated without Regular OT. An unworked Legal holiday receives 8 `LegalHolidayHours` and zero holiday OT when the employee worked on the immediately preceding **calendar day**, as requested. The lookup includes the previous cutoff's final day within the same agency; missing attendance, non-work statuses, BTR-only hours and unworked LH credit do not qualify. This is the requested attendance rule, not a complete statutory eligibility engine: scheduled-rest-day lookback and approved paid-leave eligibility are not inferred. Missing eligible holiday rows are inserted using the existing enrollment/deployment, with `Absent` status, no punches, no regular/OT hours, and no worked-day/WDO credit. Existing explicit non-work statuses remain; empty Present/Holiday placeholders receiving LH pay become Absent. Another DTR's employee/date row is never claimed. Recalculation revokes stale LH when eligibility, holiday type, or the site's Special Holiday selection changes; computed/locked snapshots stay unchanged. Unworked SH remains zero. The records response uses existing hour/status fields; paid unworked LH is included in the LH summary totals only, while its day cell stays blank without a paid-LH color or label. The day editor retains the explanatory note and stored LH amount; worked holiday cells still show their regular/OT hours. Legal Holiday dates and Special Holiday choices originate in Holiday Manager; Special Holidays apply only to sites selected in Site Policy (August 21, 2026 is SH; August 31 is LH).

**DTR clear-cell action.** `POST /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records` with `{ ClearAttendance: true, EmployeeID, AttendanceDate }` deletes only that editable employee/date attendance entry, recalculates its WDO marker, and returns the matrix cell to `Click to add`. HR-approved `On-Leave`, `Vacation Leave`, and `Sick Leave` entries cannot be cleared or manually changed from the DTR; they must be updated from Employee Status. The `No shift / manual` choice triggers this action for an ordinary saved cell; on an untouched cell it simply closes the form without creating an entry.
