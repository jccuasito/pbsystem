# API Map

## Payroll processing, finalization, and cancellation

Apply `node --env-file=.env scripts/apply-payroll-processing-review.mjs`, `node --env-file=.env scripts/apply-payroll-processing-finalization.mjs`, `node --env-file=.env scripts/apply-payroll-processing-cancellation.mjs`, and `node --env-file=.env scripts/apply-recurring-payroll-deductions.mjs` in order before using these endpoints. The cancellation migration lets `payroll_processing_posting` preserve cancelled mappings while allowing a new active mapping after recomputation; it adds Cancelled payroll/review and workflow statuses plus the prior adjustment status field. The recurring migration adds fixed-amount employee plans without declining balances and per-cutoff skip audit rows. `payroll_processing_review` retains Pending, Approved (finalized), Rejected, or Cancelled and a frozen breakdown. Workflow events retain actor name, role, department, reason, DTR status transition, and time.

`app/pages/payroll/processing/index.vue` lists computed, finalized, returned, and cancelled site DTRs. The detail modal shows attendance earnings/time deductions, eligible active loan/deduction installments, site-position contribution amounts, Approved/Ready for Payroll prior-period adjustments, and BTR coverage hours separately. BTR coverage pays the reliever separately at the site regular rate effective on each BTR date and does not rewrite ordinary attendance hours. A BTR reliever not enrolled in the target DTR or missing an effective payroll rate blocks finalization. Each attendance date uses its effective payroll rate version. Installments and site-position contributions are charged only to an employee's fixed-site DTR (`attendance_dtr_employee.IsPermanentSite = 1` and not Reliever), once per cutoff; a reliever-only DTR has none. `PauseStartDate` and `ResumeDate` determine whether an employee plan is skipped for a cutoff. Draft/For Approval adjustments, missing/non-effective rates, unsupported combined premiums, and configured allowances with undefined payout rules block finalization. Statutory contribution formulas and allowance payout are not calculated automatically; the contribution amount comes from the site-position plan. Finalization saves payroll and account transactions but does not release payslips or pay. Cancel finalization is available for an Approved, unreleased payroll; it voids payroll and receipts, restores unchanged account balances, reopens applied adjustments, retains audit records, and returns the DTR to Draft for correction and recomputation. It refuses cancellation when a receipt, balance, adjustment, or payroll has since changed.

Existing `Approved` review rows from before this migration are treated as pending unless a `payroll_processing_posting` mapping exists; they can be recalculated and finalized without mistaking an old review for posted payroll.

## Payroll adjustments

Apply `node --env-file=.env scripts/apply-payroll-adjustments.mjs` before using these routes; it installs `database/payroll-adjustments.sql` and `database/payroll-adjustment-manual-days.sql`. `payroll_adjustment` stores the employee, original DTR, later target DTR, workflow status, audit users/timestamps, and calculated net adjustment. `payroll_adjustment_line` snapshots each saved original attendance component with its date, quantity, payroll rate, direction, and amount. Attendance remains attached to its original DTR and is never rewritten by this workflow. The unique `(SourceAttendanceID, ComponentCode)` key prevents the same saved attendance component from being carried into payroll twice. Already-finalized target cutoffs are excluded from selection and rejected on adjustment creation. A rejected or cancelled adjustment can be reopened instead of recreated. `payroll_adjustment_manual_day` records each verified missing date, optional shift, and entered hours with a unique active employee/source-cutoff/date claim. Rejected/cancelled claims are released while their historical rows and amounts remain available; reopening conflicts with a newer claim. Manual days are limited to ordinary non-Sunday dates; holiday/rest-day premiums need a separate reviewed calculation. Verified missed days move from Draft directly to `Ready for Payroll` without a separate adjustment approval. Saved-DTR adjustments retain Draft -> For Approval -> Approved. Ready/Approved lines are included when the target DTR payroll is finalized; their status becomes Applied and `TargetPayrollID` links the posted payroll. The prior status is saved in `PreAppliedStatus` for a safe cancellation; otherwise an Applied record is immutable through the adjustment endpoints and a later correction requires a reversal adjustment.

The Daily Time Records list exposes **Payroll adjustments** under each row's **More** menu. The selected DTR is the target cutoff for the proposed correction; Ready/Approved corrections are posted when that target DTR is finalized in Payroll Processing. After selecting the employee, the **Original cutoff** dropdown lists earlier same-agency DTRs where that employee was on the roster, including cutoffs with no saved payable attendance. Selecting a cutoff retrieves saved payable dates and eligible missed dates. `app/components/PayrollAdjustmentsPanel.vue` is shared by this DTR modal and the Payroll â†’ Adjustments review page. Desktop uses a table with expandable component lines; mobile uses cards. Viewer accounts are read-only. Encoder accounts can create, submit, cancel, and reopen records. Admin and Supervisor accounts can additionally approve or reject saved-DTR adjustments.

# Database recovery

**DTR deduction stage.** Scheduled DTR hours are gross before late/undertime deductions. For example, DJA `07:23â€“18:02` on `07:00â€“19:00` keeps 8 regular + 4 OT, 23 late minutes and 58 undertime minutes. This contract applies to explicit form recalculation and Excel import; existing saved allocations are not silently rewritten on read. Re-enter an actual timestamp and save, or re-import the source, to recalculate an old Draft row. The Payroll Processing review screen prices late/undertime/break components once using the effective configured deduction amounts; `POST /api/attendance/dtr/:id/compute` still only changes DTR status and does not create payroll records. Request/response shapes and tables are unchanged.

**DTR late/undertime timestamps and Excel round-trip.** In `app/components/DtrAttendanceWorkspace.vue`, explicit Late-minute input on a scheduled shift sets Time In to the scheduled start plus those minutes; Undertime-minute input sets Time Out to the scheduled end minus those minutes, with overnight date handling. Hours, ND, and status then recalculate. Editing duration hours also refreshes late/undertime from the resulting timestamps. Loading a saved row never adjusts timestamps. Scheduled undertime is measured against the **whole shift end**, including its scheduled OT block, in both the form and Excel importer. Example: `07:00â€“19:00` with 24 minutes undertime becomes `07:00â€“18:36`, 8 regular hours, 4.00 OT, and 0.40 UndertimeHours; 56 minutes late sets Time In to `07:56`. Late and undertime remain separately tracked deductions; neither reduces scheduled Regular/Regular OT in the DTR. A worked scheduled duty (including referenced augmentation) keeps its configured allocations, with the site break still applied once. Actual extension and ND remain based on actual timestamps. Re-entering the gross scheduled duration preserves recorded undertime; an explicit different duration still previews its resulting timestamps and metrics. Standalone Flexible or consolidated multi-duty rows have no single scheduled start/end for minute-to-time conversion.

`GET /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records` returns corrected `dutyRows` for Excel export: zero/one-duty dates use the saved `attendance.TimeIn`, `attendance.TimeOut`, and saved shift; dates with multiple biometric duties retain each `attendance_duty` line and its shift. Tables read: `attendance`, `attendance_duty`, `shift_code`, `employee`. The `POST .../records` manual-save and import request/response shapes are unchanged. The export retains its eight existing columns; late/undertime are represented by Time In/Time Out rather than separate minute columns. Single-duty manual corrections therefore survive export and re-import without rewriting the original imported duty. Export reads saved records; unsaved form edits require Save attendance first.

**Automatic DTR Late / Half-Day status.** `GET /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records` and attendance write actions on `POST /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records` (manual save, import, batch shift, clear/reset) synchronize `attendance.AttendanceStatus` for Draft batches using `attendance`, `shift_code`, and `attendance_duty`, within the existing transaction. Existing computed/approved/locked history is unchanged. `attendanceRows` also includes `ShiftRegularHours` (the selected shift's regular-hours baseline; maximum source-duty baseline for consolidated rows). Request shapes are unchanged. The caller is `app/components/DtrAttendanceWorkspace.vue`; its live form and backend share `shared/utils/dtrAttendanceStatus.ts`.

Half-Day applies when positive worked time is at most half of the configured **regular hours**, excluding the OT cap: 4 hours or less for an 8-hour regular block. Classification uses actual timestamp duration minus the saved break, capped by credited hours minus late; without timestamps it uses credited hours minus separately tracked late and undertime. These subtractions classify status only and do not reduce stored DTR hour allocations. ND and mirrored holiday allocations do not count twice. Half-Day takes precedence over Late; otherwise positive late or undertime hours produce the shared Late status, and clearing both returns attendance to Present (or retains Holiday). The form monitors both Late and Undertime minute inputs immediately, and the same rule applies to imported/saved rows. Explicit Absent, Rest Day, On-Leave, and Reliever statuses remain unchanged. This classification does not rewrite timestamps, hour amounts, rate snapshots, WDO rules, or Total Days. The grid adds a 3px orange bottom line for Late or positive Undertime hours and a black bottom line for Half-Day (which takes color precedence), with a Late / Undertime legend and a tooltip showing undertime minutes, while retaining the existing shift backgrounds and holiday headers.

**DTR straight-duty display rule.** `GET /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records` returns `IsStraightDuty: 1` when one employee/date contains both a scheduled duty and a Flexible augmentation duty in `attendance_duty`. `DtrAttendanceWorkspace.vue` renders that date as green `SS`; the raw duties and calculated payroll hours are unchanged.

**Site-controlled WDO and Special Holidays.** Apply `database/dtr-site-wdo-special-holiday-policy.sql`, or run `node --env-file=.env scripts/apply-dtr-site-wdo-special-holiday-policy.mjs`, before deploying these controls. The migration adds `site_policy.AutoWDOEnabled` (default on), `site_policy.SundayWDOOTEnabled` (default off), and the `site_special_holiday` site-to-holiday mapping table. Existing active Special Holidays are initially mapped to active sites to preserve prior DTR behavior; later user changes are retained. The DTR and compact print summaries expose only **WDO** and **WDO OT**. The existing attendance form displays the persisted payroll allocation in read-only `RestDayHours` and `RestDayOTHours`: automatic WDO uses fixed 8/0 hours, while Sunday WDO uses actual regular hours and OT plus OT extension.

**DTR holiday credit rule.** `GET /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records`, attendance saves/imports/batch apply, and Draft compute synchronize holiday allocations inside their existing transaction (`attendance`, `attendance_dtr`, `attendance_dtr_employee`, `holiday`, `site_special_holiday`; caller `app/components/DtrAttendanceWorkspace.vue`). A worked Legal Holiday or site-enabled Special Holiday retains a one-per-calendar-day allocation: its holiday hours mirror `RegularHours` up to 8, and its holiday OT mirrors `OTHours` up to 4. `OTExtHours` is excluded from holiday OT. When the site's Sunday WDO OT setting is on, a worked Sunday that is an enabled Special Holiday posts those 8/4-capped values to `RestDaySpecialHolidayHours` and `RestDaySpecialHolidayOTHours` instead of the ordinary Special Holiday fields; a worked Sunday Legal Holiday still posts to `LegalHolidayHours` and `LegalHolidayOTHours`. With the setting off, ordinary holiday fields apply. Sunday WDO OT itself may include OT extension separately. The day editor previews these read-only holiday fields immediately, and its row/totals preview uses the same values before Save. No holiday OT is generated without Regular OT. An unworked Legal holiday receives 8 `LegalHolidayHours` and zero holiday OT when the employee worked on the immediately preceding **calendar day**, as requested. The lookup includes the previous cutoff's final day within the same agency; missing attendance, non-work statuses, BTR-only hours and unworked LH credit do not qualify. This is the requested attendance rule, not a complete statutory eligibility engine: scheduled-rest-day lookback and approved paid-leave eligibility are not inferred. Missing eligible holiday rows are inserted using the existing enrollment/deployment, with `Absent` status, no punches, no regular/OT hours, and no worked-day/WDO credit. Existing explicit non-work statuses remain; empty Present/Holiday placeholders receiving LH pay become Absent. Another DTR's employee/date row is never claimed. Recalculation revokes stale LH when eligibility, holiday type, or the site's Special Holiday selection changes; computed/locked snapshots stay unchanged. Unworked SH remains zero. The records response uses existing hour/status fields; paid unworked LH is included in the LH summary totals only, while its day cell stays blank without a paid-LH color or label. The day editor retains the explanatory note and stored LH amount; worked holiday cells still show their regular/OT hours. Legal Holiday dates and Special Holiday choices originate in Holiday Manager; Special Holidays apply only to sites selected in Site Policy (August 21, 2026 is SH; August 31 is LH).

**DTR clear-cell action.** `POST /api/attendance/dtr/:id/records` with `{ ClearAttendance: true, EmployeeID, AttendanceDate }` deletes only that editable employee/date attendance entry, recalculates its WDO marker, and returns the matrix cell to `Click to add`. HR-approved `On-Leave`, `Vacation Leave`, and `Sick Leave` entries cannot be cleared or manually changed from the DTR; they must be updated from Employee Status. The `No shift / manual` choice triggers this action for an ordinary saved cell; on an untouched cell it simply closes the form without creating an entry.
